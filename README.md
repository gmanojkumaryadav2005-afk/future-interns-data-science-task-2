# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics Task 2

### Project Overview

This project analyzes customer subscription data to understand customer churn, retention patterns, and factors associated with customers leaving a service.

The analysis was developed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Task 2 (2026)**, with a focus on customer retention and churn analysis for a subscription-based business.

## Business Objective

The main objectives of this analysis are to:

* Understand overall customer churn and retention.
* Identify important churn patterns across customer segments.
* Analyze churn by contract type and gender.
* Understand the distribution of monthly customer charges.
* Generate business-focused insights and recommendations to help reduce customer churn.

## Dataset

The project uses a customer subscription/churn dataset containing customer demographic, subscription, contract, tenure, and billing-related information.

The analysis focuses on customer-level attributes such as:

* Customer ID
* Gender
* Tenure
* Contract type
* Monthly charges
* Churn status

## Dashboard Analysis

The Tableau dashboard contains the following analyses:

### 1. Overall Churn

The analysis shows that 1,869 out of 7,043 customers have churned, resulting in an overall churn rate of **26.54%**.

### 2. Churn by Gender

The dashboard compares customer churn counts across male and female customers to understand the distribution of churn within these customer groups.

### 3. Churn by Contract Type

Contract type is an important retention-related factor. The dashboard shows a substantially larger churn volume among **month-to-month customers** compared with customers on one-year and two-year contracts.

This indicates that customers without a long-term commitment represent an important segment for retention initiatives.

### 4. Monthly Charges Distribution

The dashboard visualizes the distribution of customers across different monthly charge levels. This provides context for understanding the customer billing profile and supports further investigation of pricing and churn relationships.

## Key Business Insights

* The overall customer churn rate is **26.54%**, indicating that customer retention is an important business priority.
* **Month-to-month customers** represent a major churn segment and should receive additional retention attention.
* Longer-term contracts are associated with substantially lower churn volume than month-to-month contracts in the analyzed dataset.
* Customer billing levels should be monitored alongside other customer characteristics when identifying potential churn risks.
* Retention strategies should focus on high-risk customer segments rather than applying the same approach to every customer.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Encourage longer-term contracts**
   Offer suitable discounts, loyalty benefits, or additional services to encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts.

2. **Target month-to-month customers**
   Develop proactive retention campaigns for customers who have not committed to annual or multi-year contracts.

3. **Strengthen early customer engagement**
   Improve onboarding, customer support, and product education to reduce the risk of customers leaving early in their relationship.

4. **Use customer segmentation**
   Combine contract, tenure, billing, service usage, and demographic information to identify higher-risk customer segments.

5. **Monitor churn continuously**
   Track churn rate and retention KPIs regularly through an interactive dashboard so that business teams can respond quickly to changes.
